Cross-Lane Strategies — Deviation + CAPAs

Given a 1:many process timeline (1 Deviation with 3 CAPAs), this page compares three strategies for deriving which cross-lane handoffs appear in the aggregated graph. Each strategy operates on the master transition table T (sorted chronologically by state-entry time, ties broken toward Parent). Tables show intermediate outputs; graphs show the Step 4 aggregation unioning the strategy's cross-lane edges with T's in-lane transitions and counting occurrences.

Timeline

Each box spans from the state's entry time (its row in T) to the next state's entry time for that object. Terminal states (Closed) get a 1-unit width.

Strategy 1 — Adjacent P↔C pairs in T

Walk consecutive rows of T. For every adjacent pair where one row is Parent and the other is a Child, emit a cross-lane edge end_state(row_N) → end_state(row_N+1).

Strategy 2 — UNION ALL of Parent + one child

Filter T to {Parent + C1}, {Parent + C2}, {Parent + C3} separately. Run Strategy 1 on each filtered table. UNION ALL the three outputs, then aggregate (start, end) with count. The count indicates how many of the three pairwise analyses contained that edge.
